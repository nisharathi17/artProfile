About the Artist

My name is Nisha. Art hasn't just been a hobby; it’s been my first language. I’ve been painting since I could move my fingers, evolving from childhood scribbles to the complex textures of acrylics and oils.

Every stroke I paint is a reflection of a moment, a poem, a line I read in a novel, or a piece of the world that needed to be captured on canvas.
